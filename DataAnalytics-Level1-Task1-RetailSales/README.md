# Retail Sales Data Analysis

## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and identify
important sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance,
and relationships between sales variables.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset overview and data types
- Missing value analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly sales analysis
- Category-wise sales analysis
- Category-wise quantity sold
- Top 10 customers by sales
- Age group-wise sales analysis
- Gender-wise sales analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Additional sales visualization

## Key Insights
1. Electronics generated the highest category-wise sales, narrowly ahead of Clothing.
2. The 46-55 age group generated the highest sales among the analyzed age groups.
3. Female customers generated slightly higher total sales than male customers.
4. Price per Unit has a strong positive relationship with Total Amount.
5. Quantity has a moderate positive relationship with Total Amount.

## Business Recommendations
1. Focus inventory and promotions on high-performing product categories.
2. Create targeted offers for the highest-sales age group.
3. Monitor pricing and quantity combinations to improve revenue.

## Conclusion
The retail sales analysis provides insights into sales trends,
customer demographics, product categories and revenue drivers.
These findings can support better inventory planning,
customer targeting and sales strategy.
